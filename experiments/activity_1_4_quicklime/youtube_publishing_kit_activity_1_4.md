### 2. Copy-Paste YouTube Video Description - 100 Percent Bracket-Free

What happens when you pour cold water onto dry white rocks? Watch this intense real lab demonstration of CBSE Class 10 Science Activity 1.4: Action of Water on Quicklime!

When water is slowly added to solid Quicklime - Calcium Oxide, CaO -, a vigorous reaction takes place with hissing sounds and clouds of steam. The two reactants combine into one single product: Slaked Lime - Calcium Hydroxide, CaOH 2 -, liberating a massive amount of heat energy. The temperature shoots up past 80°C!

Balanced Chemical Equation:
CaO solid + H2O liquid → CaOH 2 aqueous + Heat
Quicklime + Water → Slaked Lime + Heat Released

Key Class 10 Board Exam Concepts:
1. Combination Reaction: A reaction in which a single product is formed from two or more reactants.
2. Exothermic Reaction: A reaction in which heat is released along with the formation of products. The beaker becomes hot to touch!
3. Whitewashing Board Exam Hack: A solution of slaked lime CaOH 2 is applied to walls. It reacts slowly with carbon dioxide CO2 in the air to form a thin shiny layer of Calcium Carbonate CaCO3 - marble - after 2 to 3 days!
