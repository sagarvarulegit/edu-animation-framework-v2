### 2. Copy-Paste YouTube Video Description - 100 Percent Bracket-Free

Why does your chemistry teacher warn you never to point this test tube at anyone? Watch the dramatic real lab demonstration of CBSE Class 10 Science Activity 1.6: Thermal Decomposition of Lead Nitrate!

When dry white crystalline powder of lead nitrate is heated strongly in a dry boiling tube:
First, a characteristic crackling sound known as decrepitation is heard as the crystal lattices fracture under intense thermal stress.

On strong heating, the white solid decomposes, leaving behind a yellow residue of Lead Monoxide, PbO, which is reddish-brown when hot and turns distinct yellow upon cooling!

Simultaneously, thick, suffocating, reddish-brown fumes of Nitrogen Dioxide, NO2, gas pour out of the mouth of the tube, along with colorless Oxygen, O2, gas!

Balanced Chemical Equation:
2PbNO3 2 solid → 2PbO solid yellow + 4NO2 gas brown + O2 gas
Lead Nitrate White → Lead Monoxide Yellow + Nitrogen Dioxide Brown + Oxygen

Key Class 10 Board Exam Viva Takeaways:
1. Type of Reaction: Thermal Decomposition Reaction - a single reactant breaks down into three simpler products on heating.
2. Color Change: White crystalline powder turns into a yellow solid residue of Lead Monoxide, PbO.
3. Characteristic Gas: Pungent, toxic reddish-brown fumes of Nitrogen Dioxide, NO2.
4. Decrepitation: Crackling sound produced by crystals breaking under heat.
5. NCERT Lab Safety Rule: Always use a fume hood or exhaust; never inhale nitrogen dioxide gas or point the boiling tube toward anyone!
