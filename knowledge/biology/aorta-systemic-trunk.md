# Aorta & Great Systemic Arterial Trunk

The aorta is the prime systemic conduit delivering oxygenated blood from the left ventricle. Its high concentration of elastic fibers allows it to expand during systolic ejection and recoil during diastole (Windkessel effect), ensuring continuous peripheral perfusion.
