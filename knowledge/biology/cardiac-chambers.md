# The Four Cardiac Chambers & Valvular System

The human heart is divided by muscular and fibrous septa into four distinct chambers:
1. **Right Atrium (RA)**: Receives systemic venous return via Superior Vena Cava (SVC) and Inferior Vena Cava (IVC).
2. **Right Ventricle (RV)**: Pumps into Pulmonary Trunk through the Pulmonary Semilunar Valve.
3. **Left Atrium (LA)**: Receives 4 Pulmonary Veins (2 left, 2 right).
4. **Left Ventricle (LV)**: High-pressure conical chamber pumping into the Aorta through the Aortic Semilunar Valve.
