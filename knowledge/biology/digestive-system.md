# Human Alimentary Canal & Digestive System

The human alimentary canal is a continuous muscular tube spanning approximately $9\text{ meters}$ extending from the buccal cavity to the anus, accompanied by specialized accessory digestive glands (salivary glands, liver with gallbladder, and pancreas).
