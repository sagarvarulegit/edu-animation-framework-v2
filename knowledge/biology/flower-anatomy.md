# Flower Anatomy

Figure 7.7 in NCERT Class 10 Biology illustrates the reproductive architecture of angiosperms. Students are assessed on identifying male vs female organs, explaining self-pollination vs cross-pollination, and tracing post-fertilization changes (ovule becomes seed, ovary wall becomes pericarp/fruit).
