# Human Excretory System

NCERT Class 10 Chapter 5 outlines the filtration of blood and elimination of toxic nitrogenous metabolites. The anatomical architecture coordinates high-pressure renal arterial perfusion with tubular reabsorption to maintain homeostatic osmotic balance.
