# The Human Eye as an Optical Instrument

The human eyeball is approximately spherical with a diameter of about $2.3\text{ cm}$. Light entering through the protective transparent cornea passes through the aqueous humor, pupil aperture, crystalline lens, and vitreous body to cast a real, inverted image onto the neuro-retinal membrane.
