# Double Circulation in the Human Heart

## 1. Physiological Mechanism
Human circulation is termed *double circulation* because blood passes through the four-chambered heart twice in one complete cardiovascular transit:
1. **Pulmonary Circulation**: Transports deoxygenated blood from the right ventricle through the pulmonary arteries to the lung alveoli for gaseous exchange ($\text{CO}_2$ release, $\text{O}_2$ uptake), and returns oxygenated blood through the pulmonary veins into the left atrium.
2. **Systemic Circulation**: Transports oxygenated blood from the muscular left ventricle through the aorta to all capillary beds in systemic tissues and organs, returning oxygen-depleted blood via the superior and inferior vena cava into the right atrium.
