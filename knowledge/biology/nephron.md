# Nephron Structure and Function

The nephron is the centerpiece of renal physiology in CBSE Class 10. The three phases of urine formation—Ultrafiltration at the glomerulus, Selective Reabsorption along the renal tubule, and Tubular Secretion—are directly represented in this 3D model.
