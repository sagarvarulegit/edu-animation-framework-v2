# Reflex Arc

Reflex arcs evolved as an efficient emergency response system in animals. In CBSE Class 10 examinations, students are required to trace the complete circuit from peripheral receptor to muscle effector and explain why the spinal cord executes the motor withdrawal before the brain consciously registers the sensation of heat or pain.
