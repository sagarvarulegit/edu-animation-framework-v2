# Ecological Trophic Pyramid

Chapter 13 in NCERT Class 10 explores trophic ecology. The step pyramid geometry visually enforces the dual core principles: the rapid attenuation of usable metabolic energy (10% law) and the catastrophic reciprocal concentration of environmental contaminants (biomagnification) at the top of the food chain.
