# Laboratory Bunsen Burner

## Anatomy & Operation
The Bunsen burner comprises a heavy cast-iron base, a vertical brass barrel, a needle gas jet, and an adjustable rotating air collar. Adjusting the air collar controls air-fuel stoichiometry between a sooty yellow flame ($300^\circ\text{C}$) and a clean, high-temperature blue oxidizing flame ($1100^\circ\text{C}$).
