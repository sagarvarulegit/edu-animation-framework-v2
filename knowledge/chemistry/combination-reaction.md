# Combination Reaction

A reaction in which a single product is formed from two or more reactants is known as a combination reaction.
In the burning of magnesium ribbon:
$$2\text{Mg}(s) + \text{O}_2(g) \rightarrow 2\text{MgO}(s)$$
Elemental magnesium and molecular oxygen combine into a single substance, solid magnesium oxide.
