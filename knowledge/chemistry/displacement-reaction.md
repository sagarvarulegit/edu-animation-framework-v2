# Single Displacement Reaction

Activity 1.9 of NCERT Class 10 demonstrates chemical reactivity and redox electron exchange. Iron atoms lose electrons to form $Fe^{2+}$ ions that dissolve into solution, while $Cu^{2+}$ ions from the blue solution accept electrons to plate onto the iron surface as reddish-brown elemental copper.
