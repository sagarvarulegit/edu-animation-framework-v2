# Reaction of Zinc with Dilute Acid

Activity 2.1 in NCERT Class 10 demonstrates the fundamental reaction between metals and acids: $\text{Acid} + \text{Metal} \rightarrow \text{Salt} + \text{Hydrogen gas}$. The pop sound confirms the flammable nature and presence of pure hydrogen gas.
