# Magnesium Metal (Mg)

## Electronic Structure & Reactivity
Magnesium ($Z = 12$) has 12 protons and 12 electrons configured as $1s^2 2s^2 2p^6 3s^2$ (or $2, 8, 2$). 
With a low first and second ionization enthalpy, it acts as a strong reducing agent in combination and displacement reactions.
