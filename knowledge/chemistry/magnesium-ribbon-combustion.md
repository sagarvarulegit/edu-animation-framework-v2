# Combustion of Magnesium Ribbon in Air

## 1. Educational Overview
The burning of magnesium ribbon in air is the canonical foundational demonstration in the CBSE Class 10 Chemistry curriculum (NCERT Chapter 1, Activity 1.1). It introduces students to the observable hallmarks of a chemical reaction:
- Evolution of energy (heat and dazzling white light).
- Change in state and color (silvery flexible metal strip $\rightarrow$ brittle white powder).
- Formation of a new chemical substance with basic oxide properties.

## 2. Experimental Procedure
1. Take a 3–4 cm long strip of magnesium ribbon.
2. Clean its surface thoroughly by rubbing with sandpaper until the silvery metal shines.
3. Hold the cleaned ribbon securely with a pair of crucible tongs.
4. Heat the tip of the ribbon in the hottest oxidizing zone (blue outer cone) of a Bunsen burner flame.
5. As soon as the dazzling white ignition starts, hold the burning strip over a clean watch glass to collect the white magnesium oxide powder.
