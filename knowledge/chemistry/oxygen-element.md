# Oxygen Element (O, Z=8)

## Electronic Structure & Chemical Behavior
Oxygen ($Z = 8$) has 8 protons and 8 electrons configured as $1s^2 2s^2 2p^4$ (or $[2, 6]$). 
With high electronegativity (3.44 on the Pauling scale), it readily pulls valence electrons from electropositive metals such as magnesium, forming crystalline basic oxides.
