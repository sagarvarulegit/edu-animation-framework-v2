# Soap Micelle

Section 4.3.4 of NCERT Class 10 explores the physical chemistry of cleansing action. Oily dirt is insoluble in pure water, but becomes solubilized when surrounded by the hydrocarbon tails of soap molecules inside the core of a micelle.
