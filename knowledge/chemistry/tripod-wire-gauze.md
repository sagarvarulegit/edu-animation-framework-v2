# Tripod Stand and Wire Gauze

The tripod stand and ceramic-centered wire gauze constitute the fundamental heating station in chemistry laboratories, providing a stable, thermally buffered platform for reactions such as the oxidation and reduction of copper in CBSE Class 10 Activity 1.11.
