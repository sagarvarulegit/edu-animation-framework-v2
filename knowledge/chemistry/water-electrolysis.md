# Electrolysis of Water

Activity 1.7 in NCERT Class 10 Chemistry proves the chemical composition of water through electrical energy. The 2:1 volume ratio observed between the cathode and anode gas collections provides empirical proof that water consists of hydrogen and oxygen in a 2:1 atomic proportion.
