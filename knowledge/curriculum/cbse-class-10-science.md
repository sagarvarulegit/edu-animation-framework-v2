# CBSE Class 10 Science Curriculum Specification

The CBSE Class 10 Science course (Code 086) establishes foundational STEM literacy organized into five thematic units:
1. **Unit I: Chemical Substances — Nature and Behaviour** (Chemical Reactions, Acids/Bases/Salts, Metals and Non-Metals, Carbon Compounds).
2. **Unit II: World of Living** (Life Processes, Control and Coordination, Reproduction, Heredity).
3. **Unit III: Natural Phenomena** (Light – Reflection and Refraction, The Human Eye and Colourful World).
4. **Unit IV: Effects of Current** (Electricity, Magnetic Effects of Electric Current).
5. **Unit V: Natural Resources** (Our Environment).
