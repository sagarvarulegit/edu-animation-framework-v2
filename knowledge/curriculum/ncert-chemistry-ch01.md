# NCERT Class 10 Chemistry Chapter 1

## Core Activities & Practical Demonstrations
- **Activity 1.1**: Burning of Magnesium Ribbon in Air ($2\text{Mg} + \text{O}_2 \rightarrow 2\text{MgO}$).
- **Activity 1.2**: Precipitation of Lead Iodide ($\text{Pb(NO}_3)_2 + 2\text{KI} \rightarrow \text{PbI}_2 \downarrow + 2\text{KNO}_3$).
- **Activity 1.3**: Zinc Granules in Dilute Sulphuric Acid ($\text{Zn} + \text{H}_2\text{SO}_4 \rightarrow \text{ZnSO}_4 + \text{H}_2 \uparrow$).
