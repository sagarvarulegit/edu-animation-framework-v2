# NCERT Class 10 Physics Chapter 11

Covers the structure and defects of the human eye, refraction of light through a glass prism, dispersion of white light, atmospheric refraction (twinkling of stars, advance sunrise), and scattering of light (blue color of sky, red color at sunset).
