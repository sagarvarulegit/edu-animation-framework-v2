# Convex Mirror

A convex mirror is a diverging spherical mirror whose reflecting surface curves outward toward the light source. It forms virtual, erect, and diminished images for all real object positions. Because it curves outwards, it gathers light over a wide angle, offering drivers and shopkeepers a broad field of view.
