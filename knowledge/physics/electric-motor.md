# Direct Current (DC) Electric Motor

The electric motor demonstrates the fundamental conversion of electrical energy to mechanical work. In CBSE Class 10 examinations, students are evaluated on diagram labelling (armature, split rings, brushes, field magnets) and explaining the function of the split-ring commutator in reversing current direction every half-turn to sustain continuous rotation.
