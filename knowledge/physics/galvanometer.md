# Galvanometer

The center-zero galvanometer is the essential detector in electromagnetic induction investigations. In NCERT Chapter 12 Activity 12.8, when the North pole of a bar magnet is thrust into a fixed coil of wire, the galvanometer needle undergoes a momentary deflection to one side, proving that motion relative to a magnetic field induces electrical current.
