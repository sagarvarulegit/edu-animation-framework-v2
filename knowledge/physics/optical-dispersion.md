# Dispersion of White Light by a Glass Prism

Sir Isaac Newton was the first to demonstrate that sunlight consists of seven constituent colors by placing two identical prisms in an inverted combination to recombine the separated spectrum back into white light.
The dispersion arises because the speed of light $v$ in a material medium varies with wavelength $\lambda$ (Cauchy's dispersion equation: $n(\lambda) = A + B/\lambda^2$).
