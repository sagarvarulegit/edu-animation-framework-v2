# Snell's Law of Refraction

The refractive index $n$ quantifies the factor by which light wavefronts slow down when entering an optically denser medium. 
For crown glass in air, $n \approx 1.517$, meaning light travels approximately $1.517\times$ slower in glass ($v \approx 1.98 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$) than in air/vacuum.
