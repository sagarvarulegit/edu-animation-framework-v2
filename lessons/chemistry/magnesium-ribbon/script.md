# Burning of Magnesium Ribbon

## Intended audience

CBSE Class 10 students.

## Core explanation

A magnesium ribbon is cleaned and then heated. It burns with a bright white flame and forms a white solid, magnesium oxide.

Conceptually:

**Magnesium + Oxygen → Magnesium Oxide**

Balanced equation:

**2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO**

## Visualization notes

The molecular scene is a conceptual model used to explain the reaction. It is not a literal representation of atoms at visible scale.

## Planned sequence

1. Establish experiment.
2. Heat ribbon.
3. Show combustion.
4. Transition to molecular explanation.
5. Return to macroscopic product.
6. Show balanced equation.
